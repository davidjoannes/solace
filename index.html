Right outside your door

Manhattan Row

A strip of cafés and restaurants on Gen. Malvar Avenue, between Manhattan Parkway and Parkview. Coffee, snacks and a dog-and-cat café at street level.

Elevated walkways

The Manhattan complex is linked by covered bridgeways to the MRT-3 and LRT-2 stations and onward to Gateway Mall and New Frontier Theater, so you can stay out of the rain and traffic.

Getting Around

Two train lines at your door

Araneta Center–Cubao is one of the few places in Metro Manila where the MRT-3 and LRT-2 meet. From here you can reach Makati, Ortigas, Manila and the university belt without a car.

Paying
Beep card, single-journey ticket, or tap a Visa/Mastercard, phone or GCash QR at the Fast Lane gates. Fares are under ₱30 end to end.

Other ways to get here

NAIA
From the airport

Book a Grab car to "Manhattan Parkway Tower 2, Araneta City, Cubao." Travel time depends heavily on traffic, so allow 1 to 1.5 hours, longer at rush hour.

BUS
Provincial buses

The Araneta City bus terminal serves routes to and from the provinces, and the EDSA Carousel busway runs past Cubao.

Events

What's on in Araneta City

The Smart Araneta Coliseum (the "Big Dome") and New Frontier Theater are both a short walk away, so you can skip the post-concert traffic entirely. Book your stay around a show.

Dates come from the venues' published calendars. Always confirm with the official ticketing page before booking.

Sports at the Big Dome

PBA basketball

The Smart Araneta Coliseum is the home court of Philippine pro basketball. The current PBA conference runs into December, with games at the Big Dome through the season. Check the PBA schedule for Big Dome dates.

PBA schedule ↗

UAAP & more

College basketball (UAAP), volleyball, boxing and big pageants like Binibining Pilipinas all play the Big Dome during the year. Game-day crowds are huge, and you'll be walking home while everyone else waits for a ride.

Big Dome website ↗

Cubao Expo

Vintage shops, small galleries, cafés and bars, busiest in the evening.
